# Asset credits

Every photograph in `assets/guides/` comes from Wikimedia Commons or Openverse
and is reproduced here under its own licence. Each is resized to at most 720 px
wide and re-encoded as JPEG; nothing else is altered.

**On ShareAlike.** Several of these are CC BY-SA. Including a work in an
application is distributing a *collection*, which does not place the
application under the work's licence. Resizing is an adaptation, so the
resized images in `assets/guides/` are themselves offered under the same
licence as their source — that obligation attaches to those files, never to
the code in this repository.

**Nothing here may come from a web search.** A picture found on Google Images
is copyrighted by whoever made it; a coffee publication, a news site or an
Instagram post has reserved all rights unless it says otherwise. Only files
carrying an explicit CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA licence go in this
directory. NonCommercial and NoDerivatives are excluded too: ND forbids the
resize, NC binds the app.

The app shows this list in Settings → Photo credits. Attribution is a licence
condition, not a courtesy: if an image is added here it must appear there too,
and `test/asset_credits_test.dart` fails the build if the two disagree.

## Methods without a photograph

`kopiTalua` ships without one. Neither Commons nor Openverse has a freely
licensed photograph of it — the only hits are `teh talua`, which is the tea
made the same way, not the coffee. A guide is shown without a photograph
rather than with the wrong drink.
